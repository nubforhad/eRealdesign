Common Questions

Quick Answers

A few things buyers ask us most before booking.

View Full FAQ
Is every SAFE plot RAJUK approved?
Yes — no plot is marketed until its RAJUK approval, Khatian and mutation are confirmed in writing.
How does the escrow payment system work?
Buyer payments are held in a bank-structured escrow account and released only against verified construction milestones.
What happens after I move in?
Residents elect a council with a standing advisory channel to the board — issues don't disappear after handover.
