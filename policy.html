SECTION 01

Introduction

Safe Community Group ("SAFE", "we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us — whether you're browsing our website, booking a property, submitting land for review, applying for a job, or interacting with any of our group concerns.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using our website or services, you agree to the practices described here.

SECTION 02

Information We Collect

We collect information in a few different ways, depending on how you interact with us:

CategoryExamples
Contact DetailsName, phone number, email address, mailing address
Property & Land EnquiriesBudget, preferred location, land size, title documents you choose to upload
Transaction InformationBooking details, payment records, escrow correspondence
Employment ApplicationsCV, work history, references (if you apply through our Careers page)
Technical DataIP address, browser type, device information, pages visited
SECTION 03

How We Use Your Data

  • To respond to property, land or partnership enquiries you submit to us.
  • To process bookings, payments, and escrow-related communication.
  • To verify land ownership documents submitted through our Land Wanted programme.
  • To evaluate job applications submitted through our Careers page.
  • To send updates about your booking, tenancy, or resident council matters.
  • To improve our website and understand how visitors use it.
We do not use your personal data to make automated decisions that would significantly affect you without human review.
SECTION 04

Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to keep the site functional and to understand how it's used. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some site features may not work as intended if you do.

Essential Analytics Preference
SECTION 05

Sharing With Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with:

  • Our escrow-partner bank, strictly for processing and verifying payments.
  • Legal and audit partners, where necessary for title verification or compliance review.
  • Government bodies such as RAJUK or RERA, where required for registration or regulatory approval.
  • Service providers who support our website, email, or customer communication — bound by confidentiality obligations.
SECTION 06

Data Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure — including restricted internal access to sensitive documents such as title papers and payment records. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

SECTION 07

Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose it was collected for — including any legal, accounting or regulatory retention requirements (for example, transaction and audit records tied to a property purchase). Job applications not selected are retained in our talent pool for up to 12 months unless you ask us to remove them sooner.

SECTION 08

Your Rights

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal or contractual retention requirements.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communication at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 11 below.

SECTION 09

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults engaging in property, land or employment matters. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

SECTION 10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

SECTION 11

Contact Us
