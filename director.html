How We Govern

Board Governance & Values

The SAFE board operates under a governance framework designed to protect homebuyers at every decision point — from land acquisition to community handover.

Transparent Decision-Making

All board resolutions are documented and shared with resident councils within 30 days. No off-record property deals.

Independent Oversight

At least one independent director with no operational stake reviews every major project approval and financial audit.

Buyer-First Compliance

Escrow-protected payments, RAJUK-verified titles and structural certification are non-negotiable board-mandated standards.

Resident Council Integration

Every handed-over community elects a resident council that has a formal advisory channel to the board.

Annual Third-Party Audit

Financials and project quality are audited yearly by an independent CA firm, with results published to all stakeholders.

Ethical Land Acquisition

No project begins on disputed land. Every plot is verified for clear title, proper mutation and government approval before acquisition.
