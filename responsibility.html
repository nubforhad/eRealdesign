Our Approach

Four Focus Areas

We concentrate our giving where it compounds — education, health, environment and emergency welfare — rather than spreading thin across everything at once.

Education

Scholarships and school support for children of resident and staff families.

Healthcare

Free health camps and subsidised medicine through SAFE Pharma outlets.

Environment

Tree plantation and green-corridor upkeep across every SAFE community.

Emergency Welfare

Disaster relief funds for flood and cyclone-affected families near our sites.

Our CSR Arm

SAFE Foundation

Charity & Community Welfare

The group's dedicated arm for giving back.

SAFE Foundation runs our scholarship programme, healthcare-access initiatives and disaster-relief response — funded through a fixed percentage of group profits and matched by voluntary staff contributions. It operates under the same board oversight as every other SAFE concern, with its accounts audited annually alongside the rest of the group.

Our Journey

CSR Milestones

From an informal staff welfare fund to a fully audited foundation.

2015
Staff Welfare Fund Established
An informal fund to support staff during medical emergencies.
2019
First Scholarship Batch
32 children of resident and staff families received their first SAFE scholarships.
2022
SAFE Foundation Formalised
The fund became a formally registered foundation with its own audited accounts.
2024
Green Corridor Initiative Launched
Tree plantation drives began across all active and completed communities.

৳1.2 Cr+ in Cumulative Relief
