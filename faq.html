General
Q01 What areas does SAFE Community Group operate in?
We currently operate across Dhaka — including Purbachal, Bashundhara, Uttara and Demra — with our first Chattogram community, SAFE Harbourview, now under construction.
Q02 Is SAFE Community Group only in real estate?
No. Real estate is our founding concern, but the group also runs SAFE Al-Amin Madrasha, SAFE Pharma, SAFE Mart, SAFE Foundation and SAFE Media, all under the same board governance.
Q03 How long has SAFE been operating?
Since 2008 — starting with a single verified plot in Demra and growing to over 600 homes delivered across active and completed communities.
Q04 How can I contact the board directly?
Governance and partnership queries can be sent to board@safecommunitygroup.com, or you can visit our office at Anowar Plaza, Demra, Dhaka.
Buying Process
Q05 How do I book a site visit?
Fill out the contact form on any venture page, or call our sales line — an advisor will confirm a visit slot within 48 hours.
Q06 Can I verify the land title myself before booking?
Yes — we share the Khatian, mutation record and RAJUK approval upfront so you or your lawyer can verify independently before any booking money changes hands.
Q07 What is the typical booking-to-handover timeline?
It varies by project stage — an ongoing venture may hand over in 12–24 months, while an upcoming one is quoted with an estimated handover year on its site record.
Q08 Do you offer installment payment plans?
Yes, most units offer a booking amount followed by construction-linked installments, all tracked through the escrow account.
Payments & Escrow
Q09 What is the escrow system and why does SAFE use it?
Buyer payments are held in a bank-structured escrow account rather than a company account, and released only against verified construction milestones — protecting your money if anything goes wrong mid-project.
Q10 Are there hidden charges beyond the listed price?
No. Registration costs, utility connection fees and service charges are itemised in the sales agreement before you sign — nothing is added after booking.
Q11 What happens to my payment if a project is delayed?
Because funds sit in escrow and release against milestones, a delay pauses further releases rather than exposing your full payment — and the resident council is notified of any schedule change.
Legal & Title
Q12 Is every SAFE plot RAJUK approved?
Yes — no plot is marketed until its RAJUK approval, Khatian and mutation are confirmed in writing. This is a board-mandated rule, not a marketing claim.
Q13 Who handles registration paperwork?
Our in-house legal team prepares and files registration documents, though buyers are always welcome to bring their own lawyer to review anything before signing.
Q14 Can I request a certified copy of the audit or RERA registration?
Yes — attested copies of any certification can be requested through our Certifications page or by emailing compliance@safecommunitygroup.com.
After Handover
Q15 What is a resident council and how is it formed?
Once a community is handed over, residents elect a council that has a formal, standing advisory channel to the SAFE board — not just a suggestion box.
Q16 How do I report a maintenance issue after moving in?
Raise it with your resident council first — unresolved issues escalate directly to SAFE's facilities team with a tracked response timeline.
Q17 Is there a warranty period after handover?
Yes — structural and major-fixture issues are covered under a defect liability period detailed in your handover documents, typically 12 months from possession.
