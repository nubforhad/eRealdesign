Corporate Overview

One Group, Six Concerns, a Single Standard

"Our business isn't measured in square feet sold — it's measured in families who never had to call a lawyer."

SAFE Community Group is a diversified Bangladeshi enterprise headquartered in Demra, Dhaka, with operating concerns across real estate development, education, healthcare, retail commerce, media and community welfare. Founded in 2008 as a single-plot land venture, the group has grown into a six-concern holding structured around a single governance principle: every transaction must be verifiable, every title must be clean, and every promise must be in writing.

The parent entity oversees capital allocation, audit, legal and brand standards across all concerns, while each subsidiary operates with its own management team, P&L responsibility and quarterly board reporting. An independent director and an external CA firm provide oversight on top of operational management.

Governance Framework

How SAFE Stays Accountable

Four governance pillars ensure every concern operates under the same disclosure and audit discipline.

Board Charter

Written charter defines board composition, quorum, conflict-of-interest rules and the role of the independent director.

Annual External Audit

ICAB-member firm audits each concern individually and the group on consolidation, with results published annually.

Escrow Discipline

All buyer funds flow through bank-structured escrow accounts releasing only against verified construction milestones.

Resident Council Channel

Each handed-over community elects a council with a standing quarterly meeting with the Managing Director.
