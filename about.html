Our Story

Started With One Verified Plot

"We didn't set out to be the biggest developer in Dhaka. We set out to be the one families didn't have to double-check."

In 2008, SAFE Community Group began with a single plot of land in Demra — fully verified, fully mutated, and fully disclosed to its first buyers before a single brick was laid. That decision to lead with paperwork, not promises, became the standard for everything that followed.

Today the group has grown into six concerns spanning real estate, education, healthcare and commerce, but the founding test hasn't moved: would we hand this key to our own family? If the answer isn't an unqualified yes, the project doesn't go to market.

Our Mission

To deliver verified, certified homes and community infrastructure that homebuyers can trust without needing a lawyer to double-check us.

Our Vision

A Bangladesh where "developer-built" is synonymous with dependable — where no family has to gamble on a title or a foundation.

Our Promise

Escrow-protected payments, RAJUK-verified titles, structural certification and a resident council with a direct line to the board.

Our Journey

Seventeen Years, Documented

Every milestone below is a matter of public record — RAJUK filings, handover certificates and audited financials, not marketing copy.

2008
First Verified Plot Acquired
SAFE Community Group registers its first land parcel in Demra with clear title and full mutation before any sale.
2012
First Community Handed Over
SAFE Ivory Heights delivers its first 56 units, with an elected resident council formed within the first year.
2016
Independent Board Oversight Introduced
The board formally appoints its first independent director with no operational stake in the company.
2019
Escrow Payment System Launched
All buyer payments move to a bank-structured escrow system, closing the door on off-record deals.
2022
Group Expands Beyond Real Estate
SAFE Al-Amin Madrasha and SAFE Pharma open their first sites within existing SAFE communities.
2024
First Chattogram Community
SAFE Harbourview breaks ground, marking the group's first venture outside Dhaka.
2026
Six Concerns, One Standard
The SAFE Group now operates across real estate, education, healthcare, commerce, media and community welfare.
What We Stand On

Our Core Values

Six principles the board holds every project, and every concern under the SAFE name, to.

Verified First

No land is marketed before its title, mutation and RAJUK approval are confirmed in writing.

Transparent Dealing

Every resolution, price change and payment schedule is documented and shared, not verbal.

Community-Led

Resident councils aren't a courtesy — they have a standing channel to raise issues with the board.

Responsible Building

Green corridors, walkability and civic amenity space are reviewed into every masterplan, not added after.

Independently Audited

An outside CA firm reviews financials and construction quality every year, results published in full.

Family Over Margin

If a shortcut would raise profit but lower trust, the board's answer has stayed the same since 2008: no.
