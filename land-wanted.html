Two Ways to Work With Us

Partnership Models

Outright Purchase

Sell your land directly to SAFE Community Group for an agreed lump-sum price, paid in full at registration.

  • One-time payment, no ongoing involvement required
  • Payment released through escrow upon registration
  • Best for owners who want a clean, immediate exit

Joint Venture (Ratio Sharing)

Retain ownership stake and receive a share of developed units or sale proceeds once the project is complete.

  • Typical ratio: 60:40 or 55:45, depending on plot size and location
  • No construction cost borne by the landowner
  • Best for owners seeking long-term value over lump-sum cash
What We're Looking For

Land Criteria

Your land doesn't need to meet every point below — but the more that apply, the faster we can move to a site visit.

Minimum Size
3 Katha or larger for residential; 5+ Katha for mixed-use plots.
Road Access
Minimum 20ft approach road; 40ft+ preferred for larger developments.
Clear Title
Registered Khatian with completed mutation and no ongoing legal dispute.
RAJUK / Authority Zone
Located within an approved planning zone (RAJUK, RAJUK-adjacent, or municipal jurisdiction).
Flood-Free Elevation
Land above seasonal flood level, or feasible for cost-effective land-filling.
Single or Few Owners
Fewer co-owners speeds up agreement — large heir groups are still welcome, just slower.

Common Questions

For Landowners

A few things owners ask us before submitting their land.

How long does the review process take?
Initial review takes 3–5 business days. If your land fits our criteria, a site visit is typically arranged within two weeks.
Do I need a lawyer before I submit?
Not to submit — but we recommend having your own legal counsel review any offer or agreement before signing, just as we recommend to our buyers.
Can multiple co-owners submit one plot together?
Yes. One representative can submit on behalf of all co-owners, though all owners will need to be party to the final agreement.
Is there a fee to submit my land for review?
No. Submission and initial review are completely free — you only proceed to costs (if any, e.g. registration) once you accept a specific agreement.
