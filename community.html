Governance, Not Just Guards

The Resident Council

Elected, Not Appointed

A standing voice on the board, not a suggestion box.

Once a community is handed over, residents elect a council with a formal, ongoing advisory channel to the SAFE board — not a symbolic gesture, but a working relationship that outlasts the sales process.

  • Elections held within the first year of handover
  • Direct escalation path for maintenance and facility issues
  • Receives the annual audit summary alongside the board
