Booking & Payment Terms

A booking is confirmed only upon receipt of the booking amount specified in the applicable sales agreement, along with a signed copy of the agreement itself. Installment schedules, where applicable, are outlined in the agreement and are linked to verified construction milestones.

  • All payments must be made through the designated escrow account or approved bank channel — cash payments outside this channel are not accepted or recognised by SAFE.
  • Late installment payments may attract a grace period as specified in the sales agreement, beyond which a delay charge may apply.
  • Prices quoted are exclusive of registration, mutation and government fees unless explicitly stated otherwise.
SECTION 05

Escrow & Refund Policy

Buyer and landowner payments are held in a bank-structured escrow account and released to SAFE only against independently verified construction or agreement milestones. This structure is designed to protect payments in the event of a project delay or dispute.

Refund requests are evaluated case by case, in line with the cancellation terms of the specific sales agreement. Approved refunds are processed through the same escrow channel, minus any non-refundable administrative or registration costs already incurred.

SECTION 06

Cancellation Policy

  • Cancellations within 15 days of booking are eligible for a refund of the booking amount, less a processing fee, as detailed in the sales agreement.
  • Cancellations after 15 days follow the staggered refund schedule specified in the individual sales agreement, which may include forfeiture of a portion of payments already made.
  • SAFE reserves the right to cancel a booking where payment obligations are not met within the agreed grace period, following written notice to the buyer.
