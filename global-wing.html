What We Do

Our Services

From your first consultation to landing at your destination, SAFE Global Wings manages the paperwork, verification and logistics so you don't have to navigate it alone.

Overseas Job Placement

Verified job orders from licensed employers across the Middle East, Southeast Asia and Europe.

Work Visa Processing

End-to-end visa filing and embassy coordination, tracked at every stage.

Document Verification

Attestation and verification of certificates, contracts and identity documents.

Pre-Departure Training

Orientation on workplace rights, local law and basic language for your destination country.

Post-Placement Support

A support line for workers abroad if contract or workplace issues arise.
